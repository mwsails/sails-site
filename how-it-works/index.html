What Are the Three SAILS Agents?

A CRO that diagnoses, a VP of Sales that teaches, an Enablement Lead that builds. Always in that order.

Three agents · One system

CRO · It Diagnoses

Reads your business, positioning, and proof, and finds where revenue is leaking before recommending anything. Blunt, numeric, never consoling: your opp rate is 11 percent, segment median is 24, that gap is worth $340K.

VP of Sales · It Teaches

Takes the CRO's diagnosis and builds the framework: ICP, messaging, pipeline stages, discovery and demo structure, objection handling, coaching plans. Nothing gets asked twice. Patient but exacting, the same way a real VP of Sales runs a pipeline review.

Enablement Lead · It Builds

Takes what the VP of Sales agent taught and builds the decks, one-pagers, and sequences a team runs on day one, matched to your brand. Practical, fast, on-format.

Each agent does one job, never another's. The CRO never builds a deck. The Enablement Lead never diagnoses. That division is deliberate: it's the same reason a real revenue org has a CRO, a VP of Sales, and an enablement function instead of one person doing all three badly.

Assess. Build. Coach. Continuous, not phased.

CRO

SAILS runs continuously, not on a clock. Assess, Build, and Coach aren't phases you finish, they're the loop the platform runs every time your business changes. Change your ICP in month one and everything downstream, messaging, discovery, sequences, knows it. That's the platform pitch: the agents are locked to your context, so they compound instead of repeating.
