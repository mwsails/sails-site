Build Track or Scale Track?

Two starting points, one platform.

Two tracks · One platform

Build Track

Founder-led teams with no system yet. You've closed 10, 20, 50 deals yourself, but what works lives in your head, not in anything a new hire could run. SAILS extracts what you already know and turns it into a playbook your first hire can run, instead of your first hire failing while rebuilding your instincts from scratch.

Scale Track

Teams with reps in seat but uneven results. Two reps hit quota, two don't, and nobody can say why with any precision. SAILS diagnoses the gap between your best rep and the rest, and rebuilds what isn't working, instead of guessing at more headcount.

Both tracks run the same three agents, in the same order. The difference is where the CRO agent starts the diagnosis: from nothing documented, or from a team that's already running, just not consistently.
